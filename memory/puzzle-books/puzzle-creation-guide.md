## The 5 Storytelling Styles (with Grade Mapping)

### Style 1: Smullyan Dialogue Logic
**Source:** Alice in Puzzle-Land, Lady or Tiger, What is the Name, Riddle of Scheherazade
**How it works:** Characters debate, accuse, and contradict each other. Math emerges from conversation.
**Best for:** Logic Lock, Code Break, Story Puzzle types
**Grade mapping:** G3-4 (2-character), G5-6 (3+ character, meta-deduction)
**Example template:**
> Captain Kiwi and Pixel find [OBJECT] with [N] [CONTAINERS]. [CHARACTER] says: "[CLAIM]." [CHARACTER 2] says: "[CONTRADICTING CLAIM]." Given [RULE], which [CONTAINER] has the [TREASURE]?

### Style 2: Moscow Real-World Scenario
**Source:** The Moscow Puzzles (Kordemsky)
**How it works:** Everyday scenes — trains, markets, farms, sports. Reader doesn't think "math" — they think "life."
**Best for:** Number Crunch, Measure Up, Speed Race, Story Puzzle types
**Grade mapping:** G1-2 (simple counting scenes), G3-4 (ratio/proportion), G5-6 (systems/rates)
**Example template:**
> [CHILD NAME] and [CHILD NAME] are [ACTIVITY] for [EVENT]. [Character 1]'s method uses [RATIO]. [Character 2]'s method uses [DIFFERENT RATIO]. They [COMBINE ACTION]. How many [UNIT] total?

### Style 3: Everything Kids Visual Play
**Source:** Everything Kids Math Puzzles Book
**How it works:** Numbers as characters, decoder rings, emoji algebra, "FUN FACT" sidebars, mazes, jokes
**Best for:** Pattern Hunt, Shape Shift, Code Break, KenKen Grid
**Grade mapping:** G1-2 (primary), G3-4 (decoder warmups)
**Example template:**
> Captain Kiwi's Secret Decoder! [EMOJI] = ?, [EMOJI] = ?, [EMOJI] = ?
> Clue 1: [EMOJI] + [EMOJI] = [N]. Clue 2: [EMOJI] + [EMOJI] = [N]. Crack the code!
> FUN FACT: [interesting math fact]!

### Style 4: Yoshigahara Spatial/Visual
**Source:** Puzzles of Nobuyuki Yoshigahara, Coin Puzzles (Demaine)
**How it works:** Pure spatial manipulation — drag, slide, rotate. Minimal text. Visual thinking.
**Best for:** Shape Shift, Pattern Hunt, special "Hands-On" events
**Grade mapping:** All grades (difficulty from 2-move to 6-move solutions)
**Example template:**
> [N] [OBJECTS] arranged in [SHAPE]. Move exactly [M] to make [TARGET SHAPE]. Tap to move!

### Style 5: Moscow Party Trick / Game
**Source:** Moscow Puzzles Ch. X, Smullyan's card games
**How it works:** "Think of a number... now I'll guess it!" Social performance puzzles.
**Best for:** Speed Race, Coin & Dice, special multiplayer events
**Grade mapping:** G3-4 (simple tricks), G5-6 (algebra-based tricks)
**Example template:**
> Think of a number. Double it. Add [N]. Divide by 2. Subtract your original number. Your answer is [N/2]!

- **Magic squares** — 2000+ year old Indian tradition (explicitly stated in Moscow Puzzles)
- **Kolam/rangoli patterns** — literally figurate numbers and graph theory in artistic form
- **Pallanguzhi / Ali Guli Mane** — Indian traditional games = Nim/Mancala variants
- **Vedic math digit-sum checking** — connects to properties of 9

## The Puzzle Quality Checklist

Before any puzzle ships, verify:
- [ ] **Story first?** — Does the child want to know what happens BEFORE realizing math is involved?
- [ ] **Character personality?** — Do characters have traits beyond being puzzle props?
- [ ] **Indian context?** — Names, settings, currency appropriate?
- [ ] **Visual element?** — Every puzzle has illustration, diagram, interactive element, or visual layout?
- [ ] **Common wrong answer?** — Is there a tempting wrong answer that teaches careful thinking?
- [ ] **"Tell a friend" test?** — Would a kid describe this to a friend at lunch?
- [ ] **One correct answer?** — Exactly one solution (or clearly defined partial credit)?
- [ ] **Solvable in time?** — Grade-appropriate student can solve within the time limit?
- [ ] **No ambiguous language?** — Crystal clear what's being asked?
- [ ] **Mobile-ready visual?** — Renders correctly on phone screen?

## 50 Puzzle Templates (Ready to Fill)

### Logic Lock Templates (from Smullyan)
1. **Two Doors:** Door A says "[X]". Door B says "[Y]". One sign is true, one false. Which door has treasure?
2. **Three Suspects:** [A] says "[B] did it." [B] says "[A] is lying." [C] says "I didn't do it." The thief always lies. Who did it?
3. **Knight/Knave Pair:** [A] says "At least one of us always fibs." What is [A]? What is [B]?
4. **The Spy:** Three characters — one always true, one always false, one can do either. [A] says "[C] is the fibber." [B] says "[A] tells truth." [C] says "I'm the wildcard." Who is who?
5. **Day Logic:** [Character] lies on school days, tells truth on weekends. Today [Character] says "Yesterday was a lying day." What day is it?

### Moscow Real-World Templates
6. **Ratio Mix:** [Name1] uses [X] for every [Y]. [Name2] uses [A] for every [B]. They combine. How many total?
7. **Train Meeting:** Two trains leave [City1] and [City2] at the same time. Train A goes [speed]. Train B goes [speed]. Cities are [distance] apart. When do they meet?
8. **Working Backwards:** [N] friends visit a [container]. Each takes half plus [M]. Last person gets [K]. How many originally?
9. **Rate Trap:** [Character] walks uphill at [speed1] and downhill at [speed2]. Average speed for round trip is NOT the average of the two speeds. What is it?
10. **Percentage Trap:** Shopkeeper sells two items for ₹[X] each. One at [P]% profit, other at [P]% loss. Gain, lose, or break even?

### Visual/Spatial Templates (from Yoshigahara + Coin Puzzles)
11. **Coin Triangle:** [N] coins in triangle pointing [direction]. Move exactly [M] coins to reverse direction.
12. **Matchstick Equation:** [equation in matchsticks]. Move [M] matches to make it correct.
13. **Dissection:** Cut this [shape] into [N] pieces that rearrange into [target shape].
14. **Number Circle:** Place numbers [1-N] in circles so each connected pair sums to [target].
15. **Maze Path:** Navigate from [start] to [treasure] following [rule] (e.g., alternating colors, increasing numbers).

### Everything Kids Visual Templates
16. **Emoji Algebra:** [emoji1] + [emoji2] = [N]. [emoji2] + [emoji3] = [M]. What is each emoji?
17. **Secret Decoder:** Each letter = a number. [WORD] = [sum]. [WORD2] = [sum2]. Crack the code!
18. **Pattern Completion:** What comes next in: [visual pattern sequence]?
19. **Grid Navigation:** Start at [position]. Follow clues to find the treasure on the grid.
20. **Fun Fact Puzzle:** Solve the puzzle + learn a fun math fact as reward.

### Party Trick / Speed Templates (from Moscow Ch. X)
21-30. **Number guess tricks, card tricks, divisibility shortcuts, mental math challenges**

### Cross-Sum / Magic Templates (from Moscow Ch. XII)
31-40. **Star arrangements, hexagon placement, magic squares (3x3, 4x4)**

### Dissection / Geometry Templates (from Moscow Ch. IV + Yoshigahara Ch. 9)
41-50. **Cut-and-rearrange, tangram-style, pentomino fitting, area equivalence**
